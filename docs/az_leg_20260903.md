| vector | mean dV | mean abs dV | raw-policy KL | held-out loss | K | decisive | end_turn played | turns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0 | 0 | 0 | 5.226 | 10 | 23/24 | 8.8% | 25 |

| random signs, same magnitudes | -0.001 | 0.001 | 0.00001 | 5.221 | 12 | 8/8 | 7.9% | 20 |
| step, value head only | -0.020 | 0.020 | 0 | 5.188 | 11 | 8/8 | 8.4% | 26 |
| step, all but value head | -0.612 | 0.612 | 0.011 | 4.921 | 1 | 2/8 | 28.0% | 51 |

Findings:

1. Direction, not size: random signs of identical magnitude do nothing.
2. The step shifts the value level uniformly downward (mean dV equals
   mean |dV| in every row), through the trunk (0.61), not the value
   head's own tensors (0.02). The raw policy moves by KL <= 0.01 at
   every scale.
3. The shift is the outcome signal at work: the seed's value head is
   optimistic in the mover's frame by roughly +0.2 to +0.3 (held-out
   value MSE falls until the level has dropped by about that much).
   The sign step delivers ~3x the correction.
4. Search turns a level error b into an act-vs-end_turn preference:
   acting children keep the side (Q carries +b), the end_turn child
   flips it (Q carries -b). With a head that is nearly flat within a
   turn, the sign of b decides K. The seed's K 10-12 under search is
   held up by its own optimism; at the value-loss optimum K is ~7
   with most games decided.
5. Held-out loss is not a guard for K: it improves at 0.3 while play
   is already shorter. It IS a guard against the overshoot (rises at
   1.0).

### Time profile and ops (iterations 2-6)

Generation throughput fell from 370 to 170 leaves/s at iteration 2
with the GPU at 80-93% utilization and 375 W of a 440 W cap
(power-capped, not thermal), the two serve threads at ~90% CPU each
(mostly spin-waiting on the GPU), actors mostly idle in pipe_read.
Per-leaf GPU cost roughly doubled. Not explained by game length
(sequence length is map hexes + units: the 2p maps span 627 to 2352
hexes, units are a few dozen). Candidate: batches padded to the
longest leaf present (attention is quadratic in that length).
Instrumented from iteration 6: tokens per leaf, padding ratio
(padded tokens / real tokens), per-game finish times p50/max.

38 actor processes orphaned by the two earlier loop kills (az, az2)
were found idle on the box holding ~17 GB; removed at iteration 5
(throughput 193 -> 242 leaves/s the next iteration, wait stage
1514 s -> 632 s). `scripts/az_stop.sh` now stops launcher, loop,
daemons and orphans in the right order (a loop kill alone leaves
the launcher alive, and it relaunches).

The restart at iteration 6 (for the instrumentation) ran the full
suite again; `tests/test_holdout_tripwire.py::test_holdout_stall_
tripwire_exits_5` (old loop, quarantined path, nondeterministic:
passed twice on this box, then returned rc 0 instead of 5) failed,
the launcher treated it as terminal and stopped the box. Escrow
worked (checkpoint + abort bundle on HF). Box restarted, leg resumed
at iteration 6 with `LAUNCH_SKIP_TESTS=1` (resume of already-tested
code; the smoke still runs). Cost: ~40 min.

## Leg az4 (2026-09-04 00:xx UTC, running): search value centering

Diagnosis behind it: with the level cap, every step was applied at
1/8-1/16 because the proposal still carried a level component
(momentum from the early level correction), so the policy heads
moved by KL ~1e-4 per step: 60 iterations of that cannot change the
raw policy. The cap throttled all learning to protect search from
the level. So the level is removed from search's decision instead:
`MCTSConfig.value_center` = the head's mean value on the latest
held-out states, subtracted from every value search reads (mover
frame). Acting children and the end_turn child then see the same
level, and K is decided by the head's within-turn shape, not its
level. The Elo eval applies the same center to the pinned player
(`--value-center-a`).

Config: from the az3 checkpoint after iteration 7 (level already
corrected, Adam moments warm), 19 games/iteration, paired held-out
test, level cap OFF, centering ON, pool deadline 1800 s, pins every
10 iterations (raw and, every second pin, searched with centering).

Pre-registered predictions:
1. Accepted fraction 1 on most iterations (the loss test rarely
   rejects; nothing else throttles).
2. Raw-policy KL per step ~0.005-0.01 (30-100x az3).
3. K stays 5-9 with >= 70% of games decided while the level moves
   freely.
4. Pin at iteration 10: raw-vs-seed is the first real test of the
   policy channel; direction unknown. If the raw player loses
   clearly (< 35% of 40), the visit targets at 32 sims are teaching
   something the seed already did better, and the next lever is
   the sims budget or the target temperature, not the step rule.

Outcome after 4 iterations (stopped at 00:2x UTC):

| iter | decisive | K | turns | end_turn | value level vs labels | alpha | held-out delta | KL/step | center |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 14/19 | 5 | 40 | 14.5% | +0.10 vs +0.29 | 1 | +0.08 +- 0.07 | 0.0031 | -0.26 |
| 1 | 16/19 | 7 | 28 | 11.4% | -0.17 vs +0.31 | 1 | +0.02 +- 0.04 | 0.0016 | -0.24 |
| 2 | 12/19 | 4 | 38 | 15.5% | -0.45 vs -0.27 | 1 | -0.05 +- 0.01 | 0.0013 | -0.41 |
| 3 | 12/19 | 4 | 40 | 14.8% | -0.26 vs +0.06 | 1 | -0.09 +- 0.00 | 0.0011 | -0.42 |

Predictions 1 (full steps) held; 2 (KL 0.005-0.01) did not: KL
0.001-0.003 per step; 3 failed: K 4-7, 63-84% decided. Reading:
with the level removed from search's act-vs-end choice, PUCT falls
back on the priors, where end_turn is usually the single highest-
prior action, and the visit targets then teach more end_turn
(self-reinforcing: K 7 -> 4 in two iterations). The seed's +0.44
level was doing real work: it priced the tempo that ending the
turn hands over. Time stream, settled: throughput tracks tokens
per leaf quadratically (932 tokens -> 310 leaves/s, 1271 -> 156;
(1271/932)^2 = 1.86), i.e. the map/unit mix of the iteration, not
process age; RSS grew 2.0 -> 3.8 GB over 4 iterations (GC time now
logged).
