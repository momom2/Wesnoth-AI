# Arm VG3 (2026-09-02): grounding on the gate's own frame +
# measured lambda0 + checkpoint continuation metadata

User rulings after the VG2 postmortem: (1) "let the label match
the states, no matter how insignificant the difference may seem";
(2) lambda's first value from the measured movement's upper
spread, relaxed as confidence improves; (3) every checkpoint
carries continuation metadata (the controller's latest
recommended lambda and the paired-label estimates), the seed's
computed once and served to every arm that starts from it.

## Changes vs VG2 (docs/arm_vg2_leg_20260902.md)

- Capture point: STAGE-1 candidate evaluation sims -- the
  mover-frame PRE-FLIP boundary states batch_boundary_values
  grades (tools/turn_search.plan_turn `capture(boundary_sim)`).
  Both labels are derived at finalize from that state: hand the
  turn over, then (rollout) play to the end with the raw policy /
  (consistency) the production depth-H projection -- i.e. the
  gate's own stage-2 grade of that state. The trained input, the
  trust-region anchor and the dv measurement are now all on the
  frame the search reads.
- lambda0 measured: signal_profiler/run_label_calibration.py runs
  one production iteration at lambda=1 on a scratch copy and sets
  lambda0 = p90(dv)/delta (docs/design_constants.md).
- training_meta on every checkpoint ("vg2" block: lambda, delta,
  bias, sigma2, dv history, recipe fingerprint); MCTSPolicy
  restores it on load when the fingerprint matches, refuses loudly
  otherwise. The calibrated seed copy
  (seed_imit_tierb_start_vg3cal.pt) is the leg's --checkpoint-in.

Verdict: the consistency term -- the search's own depth-1 bootstrap
-- owns 99% of the update. The outcome labels (game 2.6%, rollout
0.8%) are drowned; the winner/loser split of the value gradient no
longer depends on the outcome at all (cos +0.82: the same push for
a won and a lost position), and the informative late-game gradient
OPPOSES the net direction. The head is being trained to agree with
its own lookahead. Mechanism: the residual-variance estimate
sigma2 = var(search - rollout) - (1 - V^2) is a small difference of
two ~0.95 quantities; it drifted 0.10 -> 0.067 and its precision
1/(2 sigma2) ~ 7.5 per state x ~200 states dwarfed ~1100 game
states at categorical scale. As the head converges to the search
estimate the pair spread shrinks further -- a positive feedback
loop toward self-distillation. Rationing the RATE (trust region:
dv 0.1 vs 0.83) worked; the DIRECTION was wrong because the
precision estimator was unsound.

Live confirmation: fresh_value_ce on real states 0.51 -> 1.70
(above the floor), turn-21-30 AUC 1.00 -> 0.84, K 12 -> 8/9, gate
accepts 0.17 -> 0.27, first draws. Iteration 4: dv 0.21 at lambda
92 -- the controller is losing the race the consistency term runs.

Two principled fixes follow (proposal, not launched):
(a) the precision must carry its own uncertainty: with n pairs,
    sigma2 is estimated with SE ~ sqrt(2/n) * var(diff) ~ 0.14 at
    n=96, i.e. LARGER than the point estimate -- use the upper
    confidence bound sigma2_hat + z*SE (a confidence interval, not
    a cap), which keeps the term's precision at what the data can
    support (~1/(2*0.25) ~ 2 instead of 7-10);
(b) or drop (3) entirely: (2)-only -- rollout truth plus the trust
    region -- is the variant that has never run, and the profile
    says the self-referential label is the one steering the ship.
Both keep the trust region (validated) and the gate-frame capture.

## Verdict and recommendation (for the morning)

VG3 answered its two questions cleanly:
1. Grounding on the gate's own frame + a measured trust region
   HOLDS the search-input churn (0.83 -> ~0.1 per iteration on the
   training support, 0.035-0.063 held-out). Validated; keep.
2. The mixture was still wrong, for a new and now-measured reason:
   the consistency term's precision estimator (a small difference
   of two ~1 quantities, proxy-modelled rollout noise) collapsed
   to its floor and the self-referential label took 99% of the
   update. The head trained to agree with its own lookahead; the
   outcome signal (winner vs loser) vanished from its gradient.

Fixes committed tonight (not launched):
- precision bounded by its own uncertainty: sigma2 = point +
  1.28 SE (upper 90% CI; ad5c87f) -- on VG3's own pairs this gives
  precision 0.9-2.9 instead of 5-7 (-> 500 at the floor);
- rollout noise MEASURED from 2 playouts per state instead of the
  1 - V^2 proxy (9d04c2f) -- removes the misspecification that
  drove sigma2 below zero.
Both need a recalibration harvest (~$0.40) before a leg: the
calibrated seed carries the old point sigma2.

Proposal VG4, two variants, user's choice:
  (a) VG3 recipe + both estimator fixes;
  (b) (2)-only: rollout truth + trust region, consistency OFF
      (--value-ground-consist 0) -- the variant that has never run,
      and the one the profile points at.
Either way the pre-registered readout stands (K >= 10 through
iteration 5, dv near delta with lambda NOT saturating, pins vs
seed). Cost of VG3: ~$7 (box hunting included).
