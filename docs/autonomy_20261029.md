## Rules for the window

- **No push, no CI.** Work lands on `feature/local-gpu-stage1`, the
  integration branch, through topic branches merged into it (`--no-ff`).
  Nothing is merged into `main` and nothing is pushed: both ask the user
  (CLAUDE.md, Working Style).
- **CI's stand-in.** Before a training campaign launches, the full suite
  (`pytest -m ""`, both tiers) runs on the desktop at the commit that
  launches it. Its prereg names the failures that are this machine's, and
  why each does not touch the run. The GPU tests (`tests/test_hip_kernels.py`,
  the CUDA tests of the files a change touches) run under the ROCm venv.
- **The program is the design's**
  (docs/training_design_20261019/current_design.md):
  - stage C, then P, then rounds;
  - claims every third round against the reference, in fp16 on the desktop,
    since D0 was read in fp16.
  - Principle 3 holds: the training signal is the game's outcome, and
    nothing beside the network decides play.
- **Records.** Every run gets a pre-registration with its kill criteria
  before it launches, and a result either way. Every strength claim is a
  PURE match against the reference, with its SE.
- **The GPU.** One heavy GPU job at a time. A benchmark beside a run syncs
  every batch. A display reset (System event 4101) stops the run until its
  cause is found. No single kernel or call may approach 2 s.
- **Tests.** One pytest at a time (`C:/Users/momom2/wesnoth_runs/tools/pytest_locked.py`).
- **Machine risks.**
  - A Windows Update restart ends everything, this session included.
  - Every run must resume from its markers.
- **Keep-awake.** Background job `bct1y3s7g` holds ES_SYSTEM_REQUIRED until
  about 2026-11-08 23:00.
- **Heartbeats.** Cron 6650b4be (every 2 h, at :17) and cron d59f3fed

## Plan

**Phase 1: stage C's tools on the desktop's GPU (days 1-2).**
- Map stage C's compute paths (subagent, 2026-10-29).
- Move the pass, the evaluator and the plant onto the packed trunk in fp16
  with the HIP kernels. That needs loss scaling, and chunks well under the

  - the replay identity (C0) at the fp16 floor;
  - P1's identity;
  - against the fp32 padded path on a small sample.
- Write a local stage C runner, `scripts/stage_c_local.py`, from
  `scripts/stage_c_box.sh` the way `scripts/selfplay_t1_local.py` follows
  its box script: resumable, a STOP file, below-normal priority, and a stop
  on a display reset.

**Phase 2: stage C** on stage 1's games, `C:\Users\momom2\wesnoth_runs\selfplay_t1_local`. Pre-registered, then read by the design's §6.

**Phase 3: P.** The planted step played (5,000 games), and the link match
(the sharpening slope is resolved at z 32).

**Phase 4: rounds** until the window ends: generate, pass, ladder, half-B
reading, adopt or revert. A claim every third round.

**Throughout:**
- (1) Compute: the trainer's and the evaluator's rates, generation's.
- (2) Algorithmic efficiency: fewer or cheaper forwards in the step
  search, the pass's cost, the round's size against its measured noise.

Each change is measured, one factor at a time.
