# Plan (2026-09-04): 1000x compute effectiveness

Goal, in the user's words: make the policy play better; nothing else
matters. The target of this phase is a self-play training system
that is a thousand times more effective per dollar than the one
measured on 2026-09-04, so that a superhuman run fits a hobby budget.

## 1. Where we start

The strongest player ever measured is the imitation seed played at
temperature 0 (`raw:t0`, `tools/raw_player.py`). Nothing produced by
self-play has beaten it. The measurements that define the starting
point (docs/raw_argmax_control_20260904.md, the 2026-09-04 review):

| fact | number |
|---|---|
| seed argmax vs seed sampling, 40 games | 37-3, +412 ± 97 Elo |
| seed with Gumbel-MCTS-32 vs seed argmax, 40 games | 13-27, −124 ± 58 Elo |
| lookahead of a 32-evaluation search over ~350 legal actions | 1.2 to 2.5 actions; a side-turn is ~12 |
| distance of the visit-count target from the prior | ~1 nat (a truncation of the prior); Gumbel and TCS targets ~0.002 (the prior itself) |
| share of each update owned by the value gradient through the shared trunk | 0.94 to 0.99 |
| raw game on a 4090 box at $0.33/h | 12 s, about 3,600 games per dollar |
| Gumbel-MCTS-32 game, same box | 146 s, about 250 games per dollar |
| network forward, 15M net, ~1,000 tokens, bf16 | 3.7 ms single stream; 300-370 leaf evaluations per second under the actor pool |
| self-play games generated by the project so far | on the order of 10,000 |

Why self-play never improved the policy, in one sentence: no target
producer was ever both far from the prior and better than it, while
the value loss moved the shared trunk on ~19 coin-flip outcomes per
step. Superhuman play by the AlphaZero recipe at the current
efficiency is $200k to $1M of rented GPU time (derivation: the
2026-09-04 session, scaled from KataGo's 4.2M games and AlphaZero's
44M). The panel verdict on the algorithm designs is
docs/selfplay_redesign_20260904.md; everything older is in
docs/archive/.

## 2. The decomposition

1000x is a product of measured factors, claimed only from its parts.

| lever | multiplier | kind of work | the number that proves it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simulator and inference engineering: Rust simulator core, batched GPU server, a net shaped for the hex grid; tree reuse across decisions once the training uses trees (user ruling 2026-09-21: a compute optimization, not a training change) | 10-30x | engineering, bit-exact checkable against the replay corpus | leaf evaluations per second per box; games per dollar at a fixed search budget |
| Search over turns instead of atoms: unit-factored turn proposals, exact combat expectations, value read at turn boundaries after a one-turn reply | 10-50x in information per evaluation | game-specific algorithm design | a teacher that beats `raw:t0` in an 800-game match |
| Human prior and reanalysis instead of learning from zero | 5-10x fewer games | known (AlphaStar, MuZero Reanalyze) | games to a given Elo |
| Literature tricks: playout-cap randomization, symmetry augmentation, auxiliary targets, midgame curricula | 3-5x combined | known, cheap to test one at a time | Elo per game, each trick alone |
| A gate that never accepts a losing step | a floor, not a multiplier | built 2026-09-04 | no leg ends below its start |

Expectation: 100x is engineering plus one real algorithmic result;
the last 10x is research risk.

## 3. Rules

1. The reference player is one checkpoint under one decode, both in
   `configs/reference_player.json` (2026-09-20: `terrain`, the
   terrain-set arm, at `raw:t0+eo-1.5`; before it the seed at
   temperature 0, then seed2, then `relset` at `raw:t0` from
   2026-09-11; docs/checkpoint_naming.md). Every strength
   claim is a match against it, PURE convention, sides alternated,
   ladder maps, at least 400 games with the standard error stated;
   a headline claim is 800 games confirmed on a disjoint seed set.
   Power arithmetic: docs/box_specs.md and
   docs/selfplay_redesign_20260904.md section 4.
2. No teacher is distilled until it has beaten `raw:t0` in such a
   match. A procedure that equals the prior scores 0.5 and fails.
3. One factor at a time. Each lever has its own benchmark, its own
   number, and its own kill criterion, recorded in the doc that owns
   it before the box is rented. The product is claimed from measured
   parts, never from a stack.
4. Bit-exactness is a property of the harness: no kernel replaces
   its Python original until it passes differential tests and the
   replay-corpus sweep (docs/rust_port_plan.md). The Python
   simulator stays in-tree as the permanent oracle.
5. Internal metrics are crash barriers, never verdicts.
6. Compute runs on rented boxes sized from docs/box_specs.md, never
   the laptop; boxes are proposed with cost and destroyed at the end
   of the run.
7. Config-first, learned-or-exact: behaviour lives in config or in
   exact game arithmetic, not in hand-tuned constants.

rejected: any further atomic-action visit-count distillation at
32 evaluations, because it was measured worse than the prior's mode.
rejected: the sampling raw player as a reference, because it sits
400 Elo below the same weights at argmax.
rejected: proxies (CE, AUC, turn length) as verdicts, because ten
legs eroded with every proxy green.

## 5. Phase 2: search over turns, the research

Objective: a search procedure that beats `raw:t0` in an 800-game
match, with its cost per game measured; then expert iteration with
that procedure as the teacher.

Design principles, from the diagnosis:

- The unit of search is a side-turn, proposed as a set of unit orders
  by the policy at low temperature or by a beam over units; atoms are
  never the branching factor.
- Combat is scored by the exact outcome enumerator
  (`tools/combat_outcomes.py`), not sampled.
- Value is read once per candidate turn at the turn boundary after a
  one-turn reply by the opponent, never mid-turn where the head is
  flat; tempo is therefore priced by the reply, not by a constant.
- The value function is trained on its own trunk, offline, on human
  turn-boundary states and gated self-play outcomes, one boundary per
  game per epoch; it never shares a gradient with the policy.
- Distillation targets are one-hot on the teacher's turn where it
  disagrees with the base, with a KL anchor to the base elsewhere;
  step size is set by held-out behaviour, acceptance by games.

First measurement before any design is built (the panel's step 4):
sample four alternative whole turns at 60 boundary positions and play
each out 40 times; report the fraction of positions where the best
alternative differs from the base's turn by at least 0.25 in expected
outcome. This is the number that prices every turn-level teacher.
Cost about $1.3 at phase-1 efficiency, less afterwards. Kill: under
5% of positions carry such a gap, in which case rollout-graded
turn search is unaffordable and the value function must come from
human data first.

Pre-registration is mandatory: predictions, bars, kill criteria and
cost table committed before the box is rented.

## 6. Phase 3: sample-efficiency tricks, 3-5x

Each behind a flag, measured alone against the phase-2 baseline at
400 games: playout-cap randomization,
map and faction symmetry augmentation, reanalysis of stored
positions with the current teacher, human midgame splices as a
curriculum, auxiliary targets that are exact functions of the final
state (units alive, villages held). A trick that does not move Elo
per game at the stated power is dropped, not stacked.
