Both reanalyze-family designers and three of five critics converge on the same fact: the 2x2 matrix (docs/leg5_resume_verdict_20260826.md) measured TCS-as-play-procedure at ~200 Elo below MCTS-32 **on identical weights** (9-0-31), and training distills toward TCS-refined play as its literal objective. That is the leading hypothesis for the invisible erosion channel, it has a one-flag test with no new code, and no proposed lever tests it in isolation. The synthesis fixes that.

## 2. The composed redesign

**Ordering principle.** Every lever's expected value is conditional on which erosion hypothesis is true. Two confounds must be cleared before any lever is attributable: (a) the GBC label fix (2026-08-27) means *any* new leg is the first with working event supervision — the historical −80-Elo-by-100k baseline was measured entirely under broken labels; (b) the teacher question (TCS vs MCTS-32 targets) dominates every policy-side lever. So the stack is sequenced, not bundled.

**The stack:**

**Layer 0 — new baseline.** One control leg: leg-5-resume config, fixed GBC labels, nothing else changed. This re-measures the erosion rate under the only configuration change that ships regardless. Without it, every subsequent pass or fail is unattributable (skirmish critic's objection, human-cotrain critic's objection — both independently flagged this as the cheapest missing item in all five proposals).

**Layer 1 — teacher swap.** Generate and distill with plain Gumbel MCTS-32 (existing flag, `--no-turn-search`), reuse and everything else unchanged. This is the wildcard critic's salvage and the direct test of the 2x2 indictment. Repairs imported from the critiques: `--abort-k-median 10` armed, frozen p(end_turn) slope logged, K split by turn type — because this reinstates the target producer under which K-truncation was acquired and never root-caused (tcs_spec.md:13). If K collapses again, we learn the root cause was never TCS-specific; that is information, not just failure.

**Layer 2 — human co-training B.** Human winner-move CE + human-outcome C51 value at 25-50% batch mass in the same optimizer step, via the existing policy_anchor cache and supervised_train loss parts. This is the one lever the critic rated sound. It absorbs dense-value's salvageable channel (human value replay is the only data where count and outcome decouple — the leg-5 rotation pin). Repairs applied: claim reframed as erosion elimination, not a sample-efficiency multiple; mover-frame mapping gets a fail-before/pass-after test; comparison is arm-vs-control, not vs the historical trend. Interaction with Layer 1: B runs *on top of whichever teacher won*, because its role (gradient-space floor at the seed) is orthogonal to the target producer, and its own designer conceded ~0 expected standalone gain while the generation channel is broken.

**Layer 3 — reanalyze, minimal form.** Per-touch target regeneration at *unchanged* utilization (1,024 touches, buffer 4k, updates 8, turn-full-prob kept), S=4 salts on graded edges rather than S=2 (the critic's point: √2 buys 29%, and few-sample luck noise is the diagnosed defect). One variable class: target freshness. Utilization scaling (48k buffer, 64+ updates) is explicitly a *later* leg — it multiplies value-gradient exposure on 1-bit terminal labels, the exact regime Silver et al. 2016 measured as memorization (test MSE 0.37 vs train 0.19), and MuZero Reanalyse only sustains high reuse with refreshed value targets and value loss down-weighted to 0.1 (NeurIPS 2021 supplemental). Any reuse increase waits on a value-target ruling (open question 3).

**Layer 4 — value-target work, gated on measurement.** The $1 measurement pack (below) decides whether salted MC value targets have any variance to harvest. Expected outcome per the luck-ledger numbers (ρ² 0.03-0.05): no. If it dies, value densification is limited to the human value stream (Layer 2) and the fixed GBC battery at current predicate scope — no extension, because dies/survives/ownership predicates are count-flavored trunk pressure in the measured leg-5 failure direction.

**Interactions resolved:**
- Reanalyze × skirmish: moot; skirmish is parked.
- Dense targets × leg-5 rotation: the only dense-value channels retained (human replay, current-scope GBC) are the two that push *against* the count direction or are already fixed machinery; the battery extension and skirmish material heads — both count-aligned — are rejected.
- Human-cotrain coefficient × reanalyze: B's mass stays at the value it won with in Layer 2 when Layer 3 stacks on it; if reanalyze changes the teacher's estimand, B's CE term is unaffected (human states are off-distribution by design, DAgger logic).
- Estimand: whichever teacher survives Layer 1 becomes the eval sampling procedure per the 2026-08-26 ruling — but that ruling was made with TCS in mind and needs re-confirmation (open question 1).

**Honest combined multiple.** The baseline is −200 Elo per ~$10 leg; a multiple over a negative number is meaningless, and every critic who checked the arithmetic said so. Stated properly: the stack's realistic outcome is moving legs from −200 to somewhere in [0, +100] Elo per 250k steps — a 200-300 Elo/leg swing — with the teacher swap carrying the largest single share (the only ~200-Elo effect actually *measured* rather than hypothesized), human-B carrying the floor, and reanalyze contributing 1-3x on top *conditional* on the teacher fix landing. Against a hypothetical non-eroding baseline, the composed sample-efficiency gain is plausibly 3-10x. Nothing in the five proposals reaches 1000x, and the critiques collectively establish why: re-search and reuse recycle the value head's opinion rather than adding environment bits, and the channels that would add real bits (MC salting, skirmish terminals) were measured or argued down to ~1-2x. The 1000x directive, honestly read, decomposes as: stop the bleeding (this program), then buy multiples from reuse and the human corpus, then revisit value-target densification with whatever the erosion postmortems have taught us.

## 3. Experiment ladder

All boxes: standard 4090 class (~$0.37/h, 32-core, vms_enabled=false), specs+cost to user before each rental. Verdicts are 40-game PURE decisive-only BT matches on the box's idle GPU unless stated; per-side sampling flags (commit 348bf60) used for arm-vs-arm.

| # | Experiment | ~$ | Pre-registered verdict | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | Implement B, minimal reanalyze, teacher-swap tripwires; unit tests only (no laptop perf) | $0 | none (build) | tests red |
| E1 | Measurement pack, one box, ~3h: (a) reanalysis search cost *under concurrent training load*; (b) across-salt outcome variance at ~16 anchors × 8 salts on escrowed leg-5 games; (c) target across-salt agreement, TCS vs completed-Q, ~200 stored states | ~$1.5 | none (pricing) | (b) variance ~0 kills MC value salting; (c) no agreement margin kills reanalyze before any leg |
| E2 | **Control leg**: fixed-GBC, leg-5 config, ~150k steps | ~$6 | 40 games vs seed. Records the new baseline erosion rate | none — this leg cannot fail, it calibrates |
| E3 | **Teacher-swap leg**: plain Gumbel MCTS-32 generation+distill, ~150k steps | ~$6 | 40 vs seed AND 40 arm-vs-arm vs E2 (direct pairing halves the CI problem) | K-median tripwire fires → stop, report root-cause data; arm-vs-arm worse than E2 with CI excluding 0 → TCS teacher exonerated, reanalyze demoted |
| E4 | **Human-B leg**: winner teacher from E3 + `--human-mix-ratio 0.25-0.5`, ~150k steps | ~$6 | 40 vs seed + 40 vs E2/E3 winner | worse than its teacher-only arm → B parked; mover-frame test failure at E0 blocks launch |
| E5 | **Reanalyze minimal** on the winning config, ~150k steps | ~$6 | same protocol | E1(c) gate unpassed → never launches; arm worse than E4 winner → reanalyze parked |
| E6 | **Full leg**: best stack, 250k steps, standard qualify gate | ~$10 | 40 vs seed at 100k (must hold ≥ seed − 70) and at 250k vs seed + strongest pin | 100k gate fails → stop |

**Program-level kill:** if E2, E3, and E4 all erode at ≥150 Elo/250k-equivalent, the invisible channel is none of the hypotheses on the table; stop all self-play legs and fall back to the already-approved opponent-sampling test as the remaining probe.

**Total: ≈ $36** to a full verdict (≈ $20 to the decisive E3/E4 readings). Credit on hand is ~$6.3; a ~$30 top-up is required and is itself a user decision. The extractor build (skirmish stage 0, ~$0.5) can ride any of these boxes as eval infrastructure.

Power note, acknowledged: 40-game matches carry ±64-95 Elo. The ladder compensates by (a) 150k-step arms so the historical rate (~120 Elo expected) is resolvable, (b) direct arm-vs-arm matches, (c) treating single-gate passes as "continue", never as "proven" — only E6's two-board verdict is a claim.

## 4. What we deliberately do not do

- Rejected: skirmish training mix, because its dense signal feeds a head nothing consumes while cutting the main head's terminal bits 40%, and material==outcome-by-construction maximizes the leg-5 unidentifiability.
- Rejected: GBC predicate-battery extension, because ~10⁵ count-flavored label-bits/game is gradient pressure in the measured rotation direction; current scope only, extension gated on the rotation diagnostic.
- Rejected: salted MC value targets as a shipped channel, because hyper-decisiveness + luck ρ²=0.03-0.05 predict across-salt variance ~0; E1(b) can revive it for $1.5.
- Rejected: buffer 48k / updates 64-128 in any first leg, because it multiplies value exposure on unchanged 1-bit labels (the Silver-2016 pathology) and confounds every attribution.
- Rejected: component A (seed prior inside the TCS target), because off-distribution seed queries are net-flattening on drifted states — the leg-4 ratchet class — and an aggregate sharpen_top check cannot see state-dependent flattening.
- Rejected: booking any "Nx" multiplier against the current baseline, because the baseline is negative; all claims are stated as Elo-delta vs seed.
- Rejected: bundled multi-variable legs (every designer proposed one), because three postmortems in a row were attribution failures.

## 5. Open questions for the user

1. **Estimand ruling.** If E3 makes MCTS-32 the teacher, does "eval plays what training plays" (ruling 2026-08-26) switch eval sampling to MCTS-32? The per-side flags make both measurable, but which one is *the* board number needs your ruling before E3 launches.
2. **Reinstating the pre-TCS target producer.** The MCTS-distillation K-collapse was never root-caused; TCS exists because of it. E3 knowingly walks back into that territory with tripwires armed. Acceptable, or do you want a root-cause investigation first?
3. **Value-target ruling (standing).** Fresh-root-value bootstrapping and value-loss down-weighting at higher reuse are both deferred pending your ruling; without one of them, reuse stays capped at current levels per the MuZero-Reanalyse precedent.
4. **Budget.** ~$30 top-up for the full ladder (~$20 for E1-E4). Approve, and at what ceiling?
5. **Sequencing vs opponent-sampling.** The approved opponent-sampling test probes the same invisible channel from a different angle for a few dollars. Run it before E2 (cheapest first), in parallel on the same box, or hold it as the E-kill fallback?
